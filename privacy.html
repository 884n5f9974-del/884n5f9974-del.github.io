Privacy Policy

Codex VPS Backup is a personal backup utility used by its owner.

Google Drive access and use

The utility requests the drive.file permission to create, list, read, and delete only Google Drive files that it creates. It uses this access to store encrypted restic backups, restore them when requested by the owner, and remove older backups according to the owner's retention settings.

Data stored

Backups can include the owner's Codex configuration, sessions, skills, credentials, and SSH files. Restic encrypts backup contents before uploading them to Google Drive. The encryption password is kept separately from the Google Drive backup. Google OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's VPS and may also be included inside the encrypted backup.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users. Google Drive stores the encrypted backup files in the owner's account. The owner controls backup retention and can delete the backups and revoke the utility's access in Google Account settings.

This website

These informational pages have no sign-in form, tracking, or analytics. For questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
